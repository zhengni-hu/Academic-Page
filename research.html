Research Interests

My research focuses on nonlinear elliptic partial differential equations arising from geometry, mathematical physics, and mathematical biology.

  • Mean field equations on surfaces with boundary
  • Boundary blow-up phenomena
  • Singular Liouville and Toda systems
  • Keller--Segel type equations
  • Variational and topological methods
  • Degree counting and critical points at infinity
