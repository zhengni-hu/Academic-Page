Zhengni Hu

Postdoctoral Fellow

School of Mathematical Sciences
Shanghai Jiao Tong University

I am a postdoctoral fellow (Wen-Tsun Wu Assistant Professor) in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Miaomiao Zhu and Prof. Tongrui Wang.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Justus Liebig University Giessen, Germany, in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Thomas Bartsch and Prof. Mohameden Ahmedou. Prior to that, I obtained my M.Sc. from Peking University (2021) advised by Prof. Zhifei Zhang, and B.Sc. from Beijing Normal University (2018) advised by Prof. Wen Yuan.

My research interests lie in geometric analysis and nonlinear partial differential equations, especially mean field equations, Liouville and Toda systems, Keller--Segel models, and blow-up analysis.
